Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

About this application

AstroMaster Backup is a private, personal astrophotography backup workflow using rclone and Google Drive. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive access

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to access the owner's Google Drive for transferring, organizing, and verifying personal astrophotography archive files.

Data handling

Imaging files are transferred between the owner's local storage and their authorized Google Drive account. Google authorization credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration.

Data sharing

The application does not sell Google user data or intentionally share Google Drive contents with third parties. No advertising or analytics services are integrated.

Data retention and deletion

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner chooses to remove them. Google account access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
